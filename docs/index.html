Frequently asked questions

Why can I only put three things in Now?

That's the point. A short list is easier to start and finish. You can change the limit (1 to 5) in Settings → Planner.

How do I add lots of tasks at once?

Open Brain Dump, paste or type a list with one item per line, and tap Capture. Bullets and numbers are removed automatically. You can also say “Brain dump in NowNext” to Siri.

How do I move a task?

Swipe it, long-press it for a menu, or open it and change “Where it lives.”

The timer finished while my phone was locked. Did it count?

Yes. The timer uses real clock time, so it keeps going in the background. Open the app and the session is saved.

How do I add the widget?

Long-press your Home Screen, tap +, search for NowNext, and add the “Now” widget. Tap the circle next to a task to complete it.

How do I restore NowNext Pro on a new device?

Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Is my data backed up?

Your tasks live only on your device and are included in your normal iPhone/iPad backup (iCloud Backup or computer backup). NowNext has no servers or accounts.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete all data, or delete the app.
