Privacy Policy

NowNext · Effective September 26, 2026

Short version: NowNext does not collect, store, or share any of your personal data. Everything you type stays on your device.

Who we are

NowNext is published by Palmetto Group Consulting LLC (“we”). Contact: dane@palmettogroup.ai.

What the app stores, and where

Your tasks, steps, time estimates, focus sessions, and settings are saved only on your device, in storage that belongs to the NowNext app and its Home Screen widget. We have no servers, and the app does not send this information to us or anyone else.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, this data may be included in that backup, which is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

What we collect

Nothing. NowNext has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

Purchases

NowNext Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment. We never receive your name, payment details, or Apple Account information. The app only checks with Apple, on your device, whether Pro has been purchased.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, NowNext schedules them on your device (for example, when a focus timer ends). They are not sent through our servers.

Siri and Shortcuts

If you use the “Brain dump” shortcut, Apple's Siri processes your request under Apple's privacy policy, and the resulting text is saved in NowNext on your device.

Children

NowNext does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Go to Settings → Delete all data in the app, or delete the app from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If we ever start collecting data, we will update the app's App Store privacy details before that version is released.
